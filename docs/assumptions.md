# Project Assumptions

**Version:** 0.2
**Scope:** assumptions needed to interpret the 10,000-transfer prototype

| Area | Project assumption | Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Evidence | Wise is used only as public business context. | Synthetic findings cannot be described as findings about Wise. |
| Time | The prototype covers 1 July 2025 through 30 June 2026. | Seasonality and period comparisons exist only inside that synthetic window. |
| Currency | Analytical economics are normalised to USD. | The prototype does not model live FX exposure or accounting translation. |
| Completion | A transfer is instant when delivered in 20 seconds or less. | The threshold is project-defined and may differ from production definitions. |
| Revenue | Fee revenue is the only revenue category modelled. | Interest, card and other income are excluded. |
| Margin | Contribution margin proxy equals collected fee less provider and estimated support cost. | It is not company profit and excludes payroll, compliance, infrastructure and other costs. |
| Settlement | Absolute variance of at most $0.50 can be matched unless settlement is late. | Missing, late and amount-mismatch events remain distinct exception types. |
| Support | The prototype generates at most one support contact per transfer. | Contact rate is a directional operational proxy, not a workload forecast. |
| Providers | Provider names, contracts and cost structures are fictional. | No provider comparison may be interpreted as a real market assessment. |
| Causality | Seeded patterns create testable analytical signals. | Results support diagnosis inside the simulation, not causal claims about a real business. |

Assumptions that materially affect a recommendation must be repeated in the decision memo rather than left only in this document.
