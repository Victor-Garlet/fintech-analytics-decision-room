01 · Decision at risk

Lower take rate can be a strategy.
Or a leak.

A headline movement does not tell a decision-maker whether the business deliberately invested in customer price, changed its mix, lost fees unexpectedly or simply counted two events in different periods.

“How can a payments fintech lower prices and improve transfer speed without losing control of unit economics?”
01

Expected or broken?

Separate deliberate price investment from avoidable fee leakage.

02

Fast or sustainable?

Measure whether speed gains justify provider and service cost.

03

Different or wrong?

Bridge completion and settlement views before choosing one truth.
